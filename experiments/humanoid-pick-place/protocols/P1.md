# P1 supported-block RGB position development check

Frozen 2026-09-12 before captures of seeds 720–739. This is perception development,
not a robot-control qualification or a model comparison.

Use the existing fixed camera, supported G1 scene, RGB rendering and initial
randomization. The estimator receives PNG pixels, camera calibration, known block
dimensions 0.05 × 0.07 × 0.12 m and table height 0.70 m only. Assume the block is
upright on the table and that its top face is brighter than its sides under the
current lighting. No object truth, depth, labels or scorer feedback enters it.

Development images 700–704 informed the estimator. Freeze its source before
20 fresh initial captures, seeds 720–739. Do not tune during that run. Retain every
refusal and error. Compare estimated XY privately against simulator truth and
against the old all-red-pixel centroid mapped to the known top plane. Record
capture state preservation. Report acceptance coverage, all-case success within
5 mm, and accepted-case error separately. The 5 mm target is a development screen,
not a mechanically validated grasp tolerance. Rejected estimates count as failures
for all-case accounting; do not report only the easiest accepted cases.

The estimator separates two brightness clusters, uses the largest bright red
patch, maps it onto the declared top plane, and estimates center/yaw modulo pi.
It checks projected top-face coverage and known dimensions, rejecting ambiguous,
fragmented or visibly incomplete patches. Such checks do not prove that a block
is supported, upright, unoccluded or unique. Do not use this estimator during
transport or after a fall without a separately validated condition.
