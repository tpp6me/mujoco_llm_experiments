# Frozen guarded-controller qualification G1

Frozen: 2026-09-11, before seeds 500–599. Results belong in `../GUARDED_RESULTS.md`.
Do not update this protocol once qualification starts.

## Scope and policy

One supported G1, fixed pelvis and overhead reset pose. Same generated scene,
model, block, basket, randomization, physics, independent scorer, 2 mm object
penetration limit and 25-second simulated deadline as V4. No vision, LLM, balance,
walking, real-time execution, or new robot geometry is introduced.

The conventional policy acts through `PolicyInterface`, schema
`humanoid-actions-v2`, exact-state mode. It reads only public exact-state
observations and issues the same `move`, `hand`, `hold` requests available to a
future LLM. It cannot inspect the scorer through its policy argument. Private
scoring occurs in the evaluator. Observation truth is allowed in this condition.

The version-2 guard samples 101 configurations from measured arm joints to the
requested target, holding all other joints at their measured values. Reject
non-object contact penetration involving the right arm/hand over 2 mm. This
sampled kinematic check does not guarantee dynamic or continuous collision
avoidance; finger flexion and carried-object collisions are not predicted.
The actual force-limited actuator controller and contact physics remain unchanged.

Policy: approach object with +1.5 cm X offset; descend to table +7.5 cm;
close; verify thumb and finger contacts; lift to Z=0.975 m; hold 0.5 s;
verify lift using public object state; carry to (0.19, −0.36, 0.975) m;
compute release XY using the observed object/hand offset with hand X capped at
0.235 m; lower to **Z=0.90 m**; open to **closure 0.4**; withdraw vertically to
Z=0.975 m; park at (0.24, −0.18, 0.975) m; hold six seconds. Every move/hand action
takes two seconds; the full recipe takes 25 seconds including reset. Every move
explicitly requests downward quaternion [0.5, −0.5, 0.5, 0.5].

The partial opening is a policy choice, not an API correction. Success still
requires no hand contact, sufficient withdrawal, full basket containment,
basket-floor support, low velocity and two-second continuous settling after a
sustained contact-based lift. Scoring remains independent of action intent.

## Seeds, accounting and gate

Development included earlier seeds 0 and 100–499 and three retained 20-case
probes. The selected probe passed 19/20 strict cases, with seed 401 still failing
contact quality. Those outcomes are development evidence, not qualification.

Qualification is exactly **100 sequential trials, seeds 500–599**, randomized
object XY ±0.015 m independently and yaw ±0.15 rad. Reserve the entire matrix
before execution. No concurrent scored simulation, tests or video rendering.
Record source/schema/protocol hashes before the matrix and verify throughout.

Any rejected action or execution error ends the trial without retry or recovery.
Retain it in the denominator. No action target is automatically modified.
Early grasp/lift failures also terminate and remain failures. Keep physical task
success, contact quality, deadline validity and rejected actions separately.
Strict success requires physical success, object penetration ≤2 mm, no rejection,
no policy/runtime error and duration ≤25 s (1e-6 s numerical tolerance).
The gate is **at least 95 strict successes out of 100**. No selective retries,
seed replacements, exclusions or within-matrix tuning. An interrupted run remains
incomplete; it cannot pass. Save every episode, attempt, outcome and provenance.

A passing result qualifies this narrow conventional recipe through the shared
interface. It does not qualify arbitrary orientation or workspace choices and
does not measure LLM capability. Historical V4 results remain unchanged.
