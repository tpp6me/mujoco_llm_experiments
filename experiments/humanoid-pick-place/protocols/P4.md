# P4 temporal RGB/hand-motion rigid-transform feasibility check

Frozen 2026-09-12 before new trajectories 820–829. Candidate perception development
only; the acting P2 guard remains unchanged and no temporal position drives actions.
No model/API calls. Fixed RGB plus measured hand translation/quaternion are inputs.

## Development evidence and fixed model

Development trajectories 740–742 show center displacement relative to the hand
of 7.7, 25.0 and 13.3 mm from lift to lower. A rigid grasp is therefore a hypothesis
that must be checked, not a supplied fact. Initial joint fits with random starts
rejected their transport windows. The retained implementation seeds the joint fit
from P3's current-image hypotheses, then fits one six-dimensional object-to-hand
transform across up to three views. Geometry-only positive controls include tilted
and axis-aligned rigid cuboids; they do not test rendered segmentation or physics.

The latest frame supplies 20 single-view initial fits, with deterministic random
fill if needed. Each joint fit runs at most 55 iterations. All frames contribute
equally after normalization by residual count. Score a candidate by its worst
per-frame RMS. Retain P3's acceptance checks: <=0.75 px best RMS, at least three
sampled hypotheses within 0.15 px of best, center spread <=20 mm. Centers must be
within 0.13 m of the hand and cuboid corners in front of every camera.

Require at least two frames and 80 mm of measured hand translation. Use at most
three frames, with no gap over 3 s. Unique IDs and strictly increasing times are
required. Visibility loss, a time gap, invalid data, or an inconsistent fit clears
history. Reacquisition starts with fresh observations; no stale point or extrapolated
position is returned. Low-motion and ambiguous windows produce refusals. Sampled
spread is not a confidence interval. A model mismatch does not uniquely identify
slip; segmentation error, occlusion and optimizer failure can also cause it.

Known release should invalidate a tracker. This private audit intentionally gives
no action/release hint, to challenge the RGB/hand consistency check independently.

## Fresh sequences and denominator

Capture 10 G2 exact-state-driven episodes with seeds 820–829, release Z .88 m and
closure .4. Retain all driver failures, early stops and missing frames. Freeze all
source, scene and protocol hashes before capture; verify them during estimation.
Scoring/true object poses are read only after the candidate has returned.

Use six endpoints per completed episode: lift, lift hold, transport, lower, release,
retract. Expected original denominator: 60 observations from 10 episodes; the first
four yield 40 carried observations. Transport and lower are the 20 nominal
post-warmup targets, even when loss/reset leaves the tracker warming up again.
Missing targets and refusals remain failures in this denominator.

Run three separate tracker streams per episode: original; black out only the
transport RGB; replace only transport RGB with the preceding lift-hold RGB while
retaining the current timestamp, ID and hand readings. Later frames are normal.
Thus 180 responses reuse 60 original frames and 20 altered sensor frames; they are
not 180 independent trials. The frozen-image condition is sensor inconsistency,
not an actual rigid object movement or a physical slip trajectory.

Report all responses, acceptance/error/refusal counts by stream and stage; report
warmup separately. Measure true hand-relative center drift post hoc without feeding
it into the tracker. Release/retract are actual physical relationship-loss cases.
Record capture state preservation and timing; timing is diagnostic only.

Exploratory continuation screen: >=16/20 original transport/lower targets within
20 mm; no accepted original carried center error >20 mm; no candidate on any
release/retract endpoint or altered transport image. This screen is not a grasp
tolerance or task qualification. Preserve a failed outcome without tuning against
this set. Keep the candidate disconnected from control in either case pending a
separate full perception/control qualification.
