# Frozen guarded-controller qualification G2

Frozen: 2026-09-11, before seeds 600–699. Results belong in `../GUARDED_RESULTS.md`.
Do not modify this protocol after qualification starts.

## Configuration

Use the same supported G1 scene, actuator/contact physics, independent scorer,
object/basket geometry, initial overhead pose and reset distribution as G1/V4.
Interface: `humanoid-actions-v2`, exact-state observations, 101-point arm-path
preview from measured joints, fingers held at measured positions. The guard
rejects non-object robot/environment penetration over 2 mm and makes no claim
about dynamic tracking, finger flexion, carried-object collisions or continuous
collision avoidance. Preserve all prior results and failed qualifications.

The conventional policy has only `observe` and `execute` access to the interface.
The policy uses exactly the G1 approach, grasp, lift, carry, offset-aware release,
partial opening (closure **0.4**), vertical withdrawal, parking and six-second hold.
The single motion-policy change is **release Z = 0.88 m**, down from G1's 0.90 m.
Every move/hand action takes two seconds; the intermediate lift hold is 0.5 s.
The complete normal episode remains 25 s including reset settling. Downward
quaternion is [0.5, −0.5, 0.5, 0.5]. The low-level interface never corrects targets
or chooses approach, grasp, release, or recovery actions for the policy.

The evaluator supports named `g1` and `g2` configurations, records the selected
parameters, and checks source/protocol/schema hashes throughout the matrix.
The current gate also requires the declared exact seed range and 100-trial count;
other counts or ranges are diagnostics, even if every trial passes.

## Development and reserved qualification

G1 seeds 500–599 are now development data. The G2 development probe used the
previous 20 development cases plus all 15 G1 strict failures: 35 placements,
29 strict passes, six retained contact-quality failures. These selected,
repeatedly used seeds are not an unbiased performance estimate.

Reserve **600–699**, exactly **100** randomized trials, before execution. Run
sequentially with no concurrent physics tests or rendering. Randomize object XY
independently by ±0.015 m and yaw by ±0.15 rad. Do not change configuration during
the matrix, selectively retry, replace seeds, discard failures or add recovery.

Each rejected action or policy/runtime error terminates the trial and stays in
the denominator, as does an early grasp/lift failure. Save every episode,
attempt, rejection and final outcome. Incomplete runs cannot pass.

## Unchanged physical gate

Require sustained contact-based lift, full basket containment, basket-floor
support, no hand contact, sufficient withdrawal, low velocity and continuous
two-second settled dwell. The scorer receives physics state, never intentions.
Strict success additionally requires maximum object penetration ≤2 mm, no rejected
action, no policy/runtime error and duration ≤25 s (1e-6 s numerical tolerance).
**At least 95/100 strict successes** are required. No thresholds are relaxed.

This tests a narrow, supported-body conventional recipe through the shared
interface. It does not qualify arbitrary orientations/workspace, vision, LLM
control, real-time operation, free-standing balance, walking or hardware.
