# P3 experimental monocular carried-block center check

Frozen 2026-09-12 before new trajectories 800–809. This is candidate perception
development, not a controller qualification or model comparison. No API calls.
The existing fixed RGB camera and robot hand position are the sensor condition.

## Method frozen after development

Development used carried images from seeds 740–742 only. A face-quadrilateral
prototype could not produce usable fits under shadows/occlusion. The retained
candidate instead fits the known 0.05 × 0.07 × 0.12 m cuboid silhouette. It uses
visible red/background boundaries, treating neutral robot pixels as unknown
occluders. Scene-specific yellow/cyan/slate background thresholds are declared.
Red interior pixels must fall inside the projected cuboid. A declared proximity
prior places the center within 0.13 m of the measured hand; this is not proof of
a grasp or a guarantee the block has not fallen. No table/support plane is used.

Twenty deterministic rotation starts initialize at the hand position; a damped
finite-difference optimizer adjusts translation/rotation for at most 55 iterations.
Retain physically in-front, hand-proximate solutions. Require best RMS <=0.75 px,
at least three sampled fits within 0.15 px of best, and maximum pairwise center
spread <=20 mm. Return the best center only if those checks pass. Rejections return
no current position. Sampled spread is not a confidence interval or exhaustive
ambiguity bound. Orientation is fitted internally and not qualified by this API.

No candidate is wired into PerceptionSession or control. The P2 validity guard
continues to withhold post-action 3D pose. Retain prototypes as development evidence.

## Fresh cases and accounting

Use 10 G2 exact-state-driven episodes, seeds 800–809, with release height .88 m and
closure .4. Capture reset and action endpoints using the existing state-preserving
RGB boundary. Evaluate four carried endpoints per completed episode: lift, lift
hold, transport, lower. Expected denominator: 40 original frames from 10 episodes.
Retain driver failures and missing frames in the accounting; do not replace seeds.
Freeze source/scene/protocol hashes before capture and check them during estimation.

For each original, also evaluate two declared sensor corruptions: (1) black out
columns up to and including the median visible red-pixel X coordinate, (2) a fully
black frame. Preserve calibration and robot inputs. The mask location comes from
pixels, not truth. These are 80 correlated synthetic variants, not 80 extra episodes
or physical occlusion trials. Record every acceptance, refusal, error and elapsed
time. Use private truth only after estimation to measure center error and tilt.

Report acceptance and all-case <=20 mm counts separately, accepted-only mean/max
errors, false accepted centers >20 mm, and refusal reasons for each variant. Count
rejections and missing originals as failures for the original all-case denominator.
An exploratory continuation screen requires at least 32/40 original frames within
20 mm and no accepted >20 mm centers in either original or half-occluded frames;
blank frames must all refuse. This is not a mechanically justified grasp tolerance,
and even a passing screen does not authorize control or establish orientation.

No within-run tuning. If the screen fails, keep the candidate disabled and retain
the failure. Next work must address the observed ambiguity/error rather than
relax thresholds after seeing this set.
